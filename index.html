A Letter From Matthew

From a Canadian farm to the geisha houses of Kyoto...

The story behind every bottle you've ever held.

 

Dear [First Name],

Before I was a perfumer, I was a farmer's son.

I spent my early years at Meleg's Orchard in a small Ontario town — hauling apples into wooden bins, breathing in the earth after rain, learning what it means to tend something with patience and care. That life shaped everything I do today.

The wooden box your perfume arrives in? It's modelled after those apple bins. Every time you open it, you're holding a piece of that orchard — and a piece of me.

That same spirit of craftsmanship took me from a farm in Canada to the fragrance markets of the Middle East, the flower fields of India, and finally — to the geisha tea houses of Kyoto, Japan.

What was born from that journey is something that has never existed before.
